## LinkedIn

A tasting room lease at $4,500 a month with a 3 percent escalator adds $1,620 over the year. If the renegotiation window closed in October, that is money you simply pay.

That is the pattern in our December business admin audit for Walla Walla wineries. Take a 2,000-case winery: 24,000 bottles carried at roughly $336,000 on the books, but closer to $912,000 at retail. If the cellar policy values finished goods at cost, a claim could land far short of the loss.

The checklist also covers dates that slip past a harvest-weary owner: Liquor and Cannabis Board licenses that each carry their own expiration, TTB obligations, and the Washington annual report, which is due in your entity's formation month, not at year-end. Erin Denney, CPA, our Tax Manager, works with closely held owner-operators on entity structuring if your winery, vineyard land, and tasting room sit in separate LLCs.

December is quiet enough to make the calls. By January, renewals have already auto-processed.
