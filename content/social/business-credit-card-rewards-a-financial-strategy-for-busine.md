## LinkedIn

A Walla Walla winery spending $216,000 a year on cards (grapes excluded) earns $4,320 at a flat 2 percent.

The surprise is that a bonus-category card for shipping, advertising, and fuel, about $70,000 of that spend, adds a few hundred dollars, not thousands. The bigger win is the float. A statement cycle plus grace period can give you roughly 45 to 55 days before cash leaves your account, which matters when harvest labor, fuel, and lab work all land in September and October.

Pay in full, though. A $10,000 balance at 22 percent APR costs about $183 a month, enough to erase a year of rewards on a $100,000 spending pace.

On taxes, rewards earned from purchases are generally treated as a rebate, not income. Sign-up bonuses with no spending attached can be taxable. Coding every card charge correctly, corks to inventory and napkins to operating expense, keeps your cost of goods trail clean.
