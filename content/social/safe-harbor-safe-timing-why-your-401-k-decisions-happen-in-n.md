## LinkedIn

A winery owner with a $150,000 W-2 salary can hit a 401(k) wall at about $4,500 in deferrals. All it takes is staff who average a 1.5% deferral rate.

That is the ADP test at work. Without safe harbor, the excess gets refunded to the owner, and the refund is taxable income in the year it arrives.

The fix has a date on it. For a calendar-year plan with a safe harbor match, the notice must reach every eligible employee around December 1. Plans using the 3% nonelective no longer have to send it. Either way, the design decision belongs in November, not Thanksgiving weekend in the tasting room.

Our post compares the four safe harbor designs on an illustrative 14-person winery: $18,600 a year for the nonelective versus $9,200 to $15,600 for the basic match. It also covers how to spot tasting room staff who have crossed the 500-hour part-time threshold.
