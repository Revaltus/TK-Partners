## LinkedIn

Second week of crush. You slip on a wet cellar floor, and it's an ER visit and eight weeks of limited work. The bills are one problem. The cash tied up in barrels and tanks is the bigger one.

A savings cushion helps, and a good target is your plan's out-of-pocket maximum: $8,300 individual or $16,600 family on a 2025 high-deductible plan. But it's built from after-tax dollars, and it does nothing for bills that land after the max.

Tax-advantaged tools cover that gap. A married owner in the 24% bracket who funds the $8,550 family HSA cuts the federal bill by about $2,050. And because winery AGI swings with the vintage, a year of heavy equipment depreciation lowers the 7.5% medical deduction floor, which makes timing elective care worth planning.

Our new post covers HSAs, itemized medical deductions, FSAs, and QSEHRAs for Walla Walla Valley owner-operators.
