## LinkedIn

A $29 software charge costs a winery $348 a year. At a 15 percent net margin, you need about 52 bottles at $45 in new sales just to cover it.

Most owners can name their biggest costs from memory: fruit, barrels, glass, labor, the tasting room lease. Ask about the 40th-largest cost and you get a shrug. That is where subscription creep lives.

In our example, a hypothetical Walla Walla tasting room has 23 recurring charges averaging $48 a month. That is $13,248 a year, and six unused or duplicated tools account for $3,456 of it. Annual renewals tend to land in January and February, after harvest bills and before release weekends.

The fix takes about 90 minutes. Pull 12 months of statements, build a register with one named owner per subscription, and sort everything into keep, consolidate, or cancel. Then code it all to one "Software and subscriptions" account so the leak stays visible.
