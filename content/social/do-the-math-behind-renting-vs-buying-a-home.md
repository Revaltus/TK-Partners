## LinkedIn

A $550,000 home costs about $3,974 a month to own and $2,320 to rent. The gap is easy to miss because the $2,854 mortgage payment leaves out property tax, insurance, maintenance, and the lost return on the down payment.

In the illustrative example from our latest post, that $1,654 monthly difference means the home has to appreciate about 3.6% in year one just to match renting. Over a five-year hold, with roughly 6% in selling costs, the bar climbs closer to 5% a year.

Two things make this trickier for a Walla Walla winery owner. A $110,000 down payment competes with crush-season working capital, and a business line of credit at 8% or 9% can quietly make the house more expensive. Lenders also qualify you on tax returns that depreciation and good planning have already shrunk.

The post walks through the full cost table, the mortgage interest deduction (about $100 a month in this example), and how to time a closing around harvest.

Run the math before you make an offer: https://tkpartners.com/resources/do-the-math-behind-renting-vs-buying-a-home
