## LinkedIn

A $1,500 harvest bonus paid December 20 lands on this year's W-2. Paid January 3, it lands on next year's.

That is why the early-December notice from your payroll provider deserves more than a "later" folder. W-2s and 1099-NECs are due January 31, and a change that misses the cutoff can mean a corrected W-2c and IRS penalties that grow the longer the error sits.

The item Walla Walla winery owners miss most is S-corp owner health insurance. If you own more than 2 percent, premiums the company pays belong in W-2 Box 1. Pay yourself $60,000 with $21,600 in premiums, and Box 1 should read $81,600 while Boxes 3 and 5 stay at $60,000. Skip it, and your personal return has nothing to support the self-employed health insurance deduction.
