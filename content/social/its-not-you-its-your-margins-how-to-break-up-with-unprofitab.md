## LinkedIn

Your distributor looks like the star. Your real problem might be a $7,200 event buyer.

In the post's illustration (made-up numbers, not client data), that buyer's 60 cases at $120 cost $4,200 to make, $900 to deliver and $2,450 in staff time (70 hours). Contribution: negative $350, a margin of -4.9%. A 100-case restaurant account at $190 a case cleared 45.8%.

Revenue only tells you what came in. It says nothing about what went out to get it.

February is pruning season in the Walla Walla Valley, so it's a good time to prune the account list too. The post covers a written rule (for example, a 15% margin floor for two consecutive quarters), how to reprice before you part ways, how to exit with 30 to 60 days' written notice, and how to renegotiate vendor terms before bottling gets busy. One bonus: a 2% discount for paying within 10 days of a net-30 invoice works out to roughly a 37% annualized return.
