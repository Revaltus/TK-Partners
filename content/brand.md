## Industries Served

### Wineries
Specialized outsourced accounting for winery owners, including inventory management, cost accounting, and alcohol regulation compliance.

- **Value proposition:** Accountants who understand winery inventory, cost accounting, and alcohol regulations, so owners can focus on the wine.
- **Pain points:** Tracking inventory and cost of goods across vintages; alcohol regulatory compliance and reporting; bookkeeping that doesn't fit seasonal cash flow.
- **Focus areas:** Alcohol regulation compliance; inventory management and cost accounting.
- **Typical triggers:** A compliance issue or audit scare, or growth (a new tasting room, more distribution) that outgrows the current bookkeeper.

### Agriculture
Outsourced accounting for farmers, ranchers, and agricultural businesses, including farm budgets, subsidies, grants, and asset depreciation.

- **Value proposition:** Outsourced accounting built around ag realities, covering budgets, grants, and depreciation, with year-round planning instead of a once-a-year tax filing.
- **Pain points:** Farm budgeting and unpredictable income; tracking subsidies and grants; asset depreciation and tax planning.
- **Focus areas:** Farm budgets; subsidies and grants; asset depreciation; farm tax planning.
- **Typical triggers:** A large equipment purchase, land or operation expansion, or a tax surprise at year-end.

### Restaurants
Outsourced accounting for restaurants, covering inventory, cost accounting, payroll, tax filings, and food service regulatory compliance.

- **Value proposition:** A fixed monthly fee for bookkeeping, payroll, and tax, so owners get accurate numbers without billing surprises.
- **Pain points:** Thin margins and food cost control; payroll and tip complexity; inventory tracking; food service regulatory and tax compliance.
- **Focus areas:** Inventory and cost accounting; payroll and tax filings; food service regulatory compliance.
- **Typical triggers:** Opening a new location or restaurant, falling behind on books or payroll, or unclear profitability.

### Businesses
Outsourced accounting for startups and established companies, including financial planning, tax compliance, and cost management.

- **Value proposition:** A supportive partner for setup, bookkeeping, tax, and planning year-round, not just at filing time.
- **Pain points:** No financial system or entity structure in place; tax compliance; cost management; no time for the books.
- **Focus areas:** Financial planning, tax compliance, and cost management; entity type analysis and accounting system setup through Business Foundation Services.
- **Typical triggers:** Starting a business, outgrowing spreadsheets, or needing an entity-type decision or financial planning.

### Estates & Trusts
Tax administration and accounting support for trustees and estate administrators.

- **Value proposition:** Patient, plain-language guidance through estate and trust tax administration, so trustees can fulfill their duties with confidence.
- **Pain points:** Unfamiliar fiduciary tax rules; basis tracking and gift tax questions; the administrative burden on trustees during a stressful time.
- **Typical triggers:** A death or trust funding that makes someone a trustee or administrator, or an estate plan that needs updating.
