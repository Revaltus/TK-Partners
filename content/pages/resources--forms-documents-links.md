## Everything you need to hand us, in one place

This page is a working toolbox, not a directory of licensing forms. If you're a TK Partners client in Walla Walla, Moses Lake or anywhere across Eastern Washington, you'll find the paperwork that keeps your return, your books and your payroll moving. Below you'll find tax checklists, our secure upload and e-sign steps, document lists for wineries, farms and restaurants, bookkeeping templates and a few links worth bookmarking. Our secure, paperless process is the reason this page exists. Fewer attachments, fewer back-and-forth emails, and no more hunting for that one missing form.

## Tax documents to gather for your personal and business return

Gather these before we sit down together and your return moves faster. The right paperwork also makes year-round tax advice possible: we can spot a planning move in March instead of finding it in April. This list supports our [Business & Personal Income Tax](/what-we-do/income-tax) service, and a printable PDF version of this tax documentation checklist is available from our team on request.

- Personal income tax forms: W-2s from every employer, 1099s for interest, dividends, retirement and freelance income, and 1098s for mortgage interest or tuition
- Last year's filed return, so we can match carryovers and spot changes
- Business tax forms and records: a current profit and loss statement and balance sheet
- Payroll reports, including quarterly filings
- Asset purchase records with the date, cost and invoice
- **Form W-9** (Request for Taxpayer Identification Number and Certification): the form a contractor or vendor supplies so you can issue a 1099
- **Form 8821** (Tax Information Authorization): lets us view your IRS records without speaking for you
- **Form 2848** (Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative): lets us represent you before the IRS, such as answering a notice

## Frequently Asked Questions About Forms, documents and useful links

**Q: What documents should I bring to my CPA for my tax return?**
A: Bring your W-2s, 1099s, 1098s and last year's return for personal filings. Business owners add a profit and loss statement, payroll reports and records of asset purchases, including date and cost. The sooner everything is in one place, the sooner your preparer can spot planning opportunities.

**Q: What is the difference between IRS Form 8821 and Form 2848?**
A: Form 8821 is a Tax Information Authorization: it lets your CPA view your IRS records but not act for you. Form 2848 is a Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative: it lets your CPA represent you before the IRS, such as responding to a notice or negotiating on your behalf.

**Q: Is it safe to email tax documents to my accountant?**
A: Plain email is not a secure way to send Social Security numbers, bank details or payroll records. TK Partners uses a secure digital tax process: you get a request, upload your files to a protected link, and e-sign your forms. Nothing sensitive needs to travel as an email attachment.

**Q: What records do wineries need to keep for their accountant?**
A: Wineries should keep inventory and cost-of-goods records for each vintage, plus the records behind alcohol regulatory compliance and reporting. Clean vintage-level numbers help your accountant calculate true cost of goods, support your tax return and answer questions quickly if a compliance issue or audit ever comes up.

**Q: Which offices does TK Partners serve?**
A: TK Partners has offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake, Washington, and serves clients across Eastern Washington. Our team of CPAs and client accounting specialists works with individuals, wineries, agricultural operations and restaurants. If you're unsure which office is closest, the contact page will point you to the right team.
