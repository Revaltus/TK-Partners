## Winery tax planning that follows your vintages

Wine is not a one-year product, and your books shouldn't treat it like one. Inventory and cost of goods move across vintages, and a bookkeeper who doesn't track them leaves your tax return resting on guesses. Alcohol regulatory compliance and reporting add another layer, and the numbers you report there need to agree with the numbers on your return.

Owners often start looking for new help after a compliance issue or an audit scare. Others call when growth catches up with them: a new tasting room, wider distribution, a bookkeeper who has reached the edge of what they know. Both are good moments to start.

Winery tax planning in Walla Walla works best with accountants who understand winery inventory, cost accounting and alcohol regulations. That is the whole point of working with us. You focus on the wine, and we keep the [winery books](/wineries) and the return in step with each other.

## How can farm and ranch tax preparation avoid a year-end surprise?

The worst tax news arrives in December or January, when nothing can be done about it. Farm income is unpredictable, so a strong year and a tax bill you didn't see coming often show up together.

Planning during the year changes that. We look at where income is running, then decide what to do about it while there is still time to act. That includes how subsidies and grants are tracked and reported, so they land in the right place on your return.

Depreciation is the other big lever. A large equipment purchase, new land, or an expansion of the operation all change your tax picture. Talk to us before you sign, and we can plan the depreciation around the purchase instead of reacting to it afterward.

If you need agricultural tax preparation or farm tax services in Walla Walla, our [agriculture team](/agriculture) builds the plan around ag realities: budgets, grants, depreciation and a calendar that follows the seasons.

## Restaurant tax services for thin margins and tip-heavy payroll

Restaurants run on thin margins, so a small error in food cost or payroll shows up fast. Tips make payroll more complicated, inventory is always moving, and food service tax compliance has its own rules. If your books are behind, your tax return inherits every gap.

Restaurant owners often reach out at a turning point. They are opening a new location, falling behind on books or payroll, or unsure whether the place is actually making money.

Our answer is a fixed monthly fee for bookkeeping, payroll and tax. Your return is built from books that stay current all year, and your bill never surprises you. Restaurant tax services in Walla Walla should give you accurate numbers you can use on a Tuesday, not just a form you file in the spring. See how we work with [restaurant owners](/restaurants).
