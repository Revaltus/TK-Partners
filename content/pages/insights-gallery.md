Looking for CPA insights Walla Walla owners can actually use? Start here. ## Winery accounting resources and tax advice for Washington producers

A new tasting room or a wider distribution deal is great news for the wine. It can be hard on the books. If your bookkeeping hasn't kept pace, these are the winery accounting resources for you, along with tax advice for wineries in Washington written by CPAs who understand your production cycle.

Expect recurring themes:

- Tracking inventory and cost of goods across vintages
- Alcohol regulatory compliance and reporting
- What to do when a compliance issue or audit scare lands in your inbox

Sample headlines, framed as the situations owners bring us:

- "The tasting room doubled our sales. Why can't we tell what a case costs us?"
- "A compliance letter arrived on a Friday. Here's the calm way through it."

## Farm accounting insights built around ag realities

Farm income rarely arrives on a tidy schedule. Weather, markets and harvest timing all move the numbers, so our farm accounting insights focus on planning you can do all year, not scrambling at filing time. This is the agricultural accounting blog for Eastern Washington operations.

Planned themes include:

- Farm budgeting when income is unpredictable
- Tracking subsidies and grants so nothing slips through
- Depreciation and tax planning around a large equipment purchase or a land expansion

The goal is simple: no year-end tax surprise. A sample headline: "We just bought a combine. What should we have done before December?"

See how we work with growers and ranchers on our [Agriculture page](/agriculture).

## Restaurant accounting tips for thin margins and busy owners

You are on your feet twelve hours a day. The books can wait, until payroll is late and nobody can say which menu items actually make money. Our restaurant accounting tips are written for exactly that moment.

Look for articles on food cost control, tip and payroll complexity, inventory tracking and food service tax compliance. Each one ends with something you can do this week.

Here is our proof point: a fixed monthly fee for bookkeeping, payroll and tax. You get accurate numbers and no billing surprises, so a busy Saturday never turns into a surprise invoice on Monday.

## Business tax strategies for owner-operators

Owner-operators don't need a once-a-year conversation. They need a plan that runs longer than one return. Tax Manager Erin Denney, CPA, leads our tax planning articles for people who run wineries, farms and restaurants.

Her themes:

- Multi-year tax planning that looks past this April
- How entity structure changes your tax bill
- Year-round advice, so decisions get made before the deadline, not after

These pieces double as practical business tax strategies for Walla Walla owners, with personal income tax tips mixed in for the household side of the ledger. Our tax process is secure and paperless, with digital document upload and e-signing, so the planning stays easy to act on.
