## Winery accounting that keeps pace with every vintage

If your bookkeeper has never counted a barrel, you're paying for their learning curve. Wine accounting is its own animal. Cost of goods has to follow each wine across vintages, from fruit to bottle to sale, while the same books support alcohol regulatory reporting.

Those are the problems winery owners and GMs bring to us. Inventory that doesn't tie out. Cost of goods that is more guess than number. Compliance reports assembled in a panic.

Most owners call after a trigger: a compliance issue or audit scare, or growth, such as a new tasting room or wider distribution, that the current bookkeeper can't keep up with. Calling before it happens is cheaper and a lot calmer.

As a CPA for wineries in Walla Walla, WA, TK Partners brings accountants who understand winery inventory, cost accounting and alcohol regulations. Your winery tax services come from the same team that keeps your monthly numbers, so nothing gets lost between bookkeeper and tax preparer. Looking for a wine industry accountant? You focus on the wine. We handle the math behind it.

## Farm and agricultural accounting built around real ag seasons

Farm income doesn't arrive on a tidy monthly schedule, and your accounting shouldn't pretend it does. Weather, prices and harvest timing make budgeting hard. Subsidies and grants need tracking. Depreciation on equipment can have a big impact on your tax bill.

The usual trigger is a large equipment purchase, a land or operation expansion, or a tax surprise at year-end. By then, most of the good options have passed.

Our agriculture accounting services are outsourced accounting built around ag realities: farm budgets, grant and subsidy tracking, and depreciation planning. The difference is the calendar. Instead of once-a-year tax prep, you get year-round planning, so you can ask "should we buy this tractor?" in March and not discover the answer in December.

Whether you need farm accounting in Walla Walla or an agriculture tax specialist closer to Moses Lake, you get a local team. Our Moses Lake office serves the surrounding agricultural area of Eastern Washington, and our Walla Walla office is part of the same Eastern Washington team. That means agricultural accounting from people who know your seasons.

## Restaurant books that show where your margin goes

A full dining room doesn't guarantee a profit. Margins are thin, food costs creep, tips complicate payroll, and inventory moves faster than anyone can count it. Add food service regulatory and tax compliance, and the books are the first thing to slip.

We hear from restaurant owners when they're opening a new location, falling behind on books or payroll, or staring at a P&L that doesn't explain why cash is tight.

Our answer is simple: one fixed monthly fee covering bookkeeping, payroll and tax. You get accurate numbers on a steady schedule, with no billing surprises when the month gets busy. Food cost, labor and prime cost show up in plain language, so you can see what each shift is earning.

## Tax strategy for family-owned wineries, farms and restaurants

Family-held businesses make big decisions that echo for years: a new press, a second location, a bigger combine, a change in who owns what. Each one touches the tax picture long after the purchase.

Erin Denney, CPA, is TK Partners' Tax Manager, and multi-year tax planning for owner-operators is the heart of her role. That includes entity structuring, which is simply choosing how your business is legally set up and taxed, and mapping how equipment purchases and expansion will land across several tax years, not just this one.

The goal is no surprises. You see the likely tax result before you sign, so the decision is about the business and not a bill. It's also why business tax preparation in Walla Walla works best when planning starts well before filing, and why winery tax services are most valuable in the middle of the year.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Industries served by TK Partners

**Q: Do you work with wineries outside Walla Walla?**

**Q: How does the fixed monthly bookkeeping fee work?**
A: Bookkeeping is billed at a fixed monthly fee, so you know the cost before the month starts. For restaurants, the fee can cover bookkeeping, payroll and tax together. There are no hourly surprises when a busy week, a new tasting room or a harvest pushes the workload up.

**Q: What makes winery accounting different from regular bookkeeping?**
A: Wineries carry inventory across several vintages, so cost of goods has to be tracked by lot and year, not just by month. Add alcohol regulatory reporting and the books get technical fast. TK Partners CPAs understand winery inventory, cost accounting and alcohol regulations, so reports stay accurate and audit-ready.

**Q: When should a farm bring in an accountant?**
A: Ideally before a large equipment purchase, land purchase or expansion, since depreciation and timing change the tax result. Waiting until year-end limits your options. Year-round planning with TK Partners lets you budget around unpredictable income and track grants and subsidies as they come in.
