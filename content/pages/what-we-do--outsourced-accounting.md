## One fixed monthly fee and no billing surprises

A surprise invoice is a bad ingredient in any budget. Our bookkeeping runs on one fixed monthly fee, so you know the number before the month starts. It does not swell because you had a busy quarter.

For a restaurant owner on thin margins, that predictability matters. Food costs and labor already move week to week, and your accounting bill should not join them. For a farmer or winery owner with lumpy income, a steady fee is far easier to plan around than a bill that spikes when cash is tight between sales.

In plain terms, the fee covers your ongoing bookkeeping: transactions recorded, accounts reconciled and financials you can actually read. We agree on the scope up front. If your needs change, say a new tasting room or a second location, we talk about it first, before anything shows up on an invoice. That is what managed accounting services in Walla Walla should feel like, and it is the reason our bookkeeping clients can budget with confidence.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Outsourced accounting services

**Q: What is outsourced accounting?**
A: Outsourced accounting means a CPA firm handles your bookkeeping, payroll and tax work instead of an in-house employee. At TK Partners, a team of CPAs and client accounting specialists keeps your records current, runs payroll and prepares returns, so you get timely financials without hiring a full-time accountant.

**Q: How does the fixed monthly fee work?**
A: TK Partners charges a fixed monthly fee for bookkeeping, so you know the cost before the month begins. The fee is set around the scope of work we agree on up front. If your needs change, such as opening a second location, we discuss it first rather than sending a surprise invoice.

**Q: Do you work with wineries, farms and restaurants?**
A: Yes. Wineries, agricultural operations and restaurants are core to our work. That includes inventory and cost of goods across vintages, alcohol regulatory reporting, farm budgeting and depreciation, and food cost, tip and payroll complexity. Our offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake serve clients across Eastern Washington.

**Q: Is my financial information kept secure?**
A: Documents move through a secure, paperless process. You upload files and e-sign through a secure digital system rather than mailing or dropping off paper. That keeps your records in one organized place and makes onboarding simple, whether you are in Walla Walla, Moses Lake or out in the field.

**Q: Can you handle payroll and tax along with bookkeeping?**
A: Yes. Outsourced accounting at TK Partners covers a virtual bookkeeper, payroll and tax under one team. Because the same people see your books, payroll and returns, tax planning can happen during the year, such as before an equipment purchase, instead of after the year closes.
