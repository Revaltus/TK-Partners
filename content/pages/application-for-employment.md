## Build your accounting career in wine country

Most job boards give you a title, a salary range and a button. That's a listing, not a look at what your days would feel like. If you're searching for CPA jobs in Walla Walla, WA, or accounting firm careers in Walla Walla, start here instead.

TK Partners, formerly TKCPA, is a supportive partner to clients all year long, and the same spirit runs inside the firm. Our clients grow, pour and cook for a living, and our team coaches them through the numbers. Tell us where you'd like to grow in the [application form below](#apply).

## The work you'll do for wineries, farms and restaurants

The roles here are built around real clients with real calendars. On the [outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) side, that means virtual bookkeeping, payroll and tax for owners who would rather be in the cellar, the field or the kitchen. On the [income tax](/what-we-do/income-tax) side, it means business and personal returns plus year-round advice, all through a secure, paperless process.

Winery accounting jobs in Walla Walla start with inventory and cost of goods across vintages. Agriculture accounting jobs mean farm budgets, grants and depreciation that make sense of unpredictable income. For restaurants, you'll work on food cost and tip payroll, where thin margins leave little room for error.

Whether your strength is tax accounting, bookkeeping or payroll, you'll see how the pieces fit for [wineries](/wineries), [agricultural operations](/agriculture) and [restaurants](/restaurants).

## Frequently Asked Questions About Apply for a career at TK Partners

**Q: What kinds of accounting jobs does TK Partners offer in Walla Walla?**
A: TK Partners' work covers outsourced accounting, including virtual bookkeeping and payroll, plus business and personal income tax. Specific openings change, so the application form asks for your area of interest, such as tax, outsourced accounting or bookkeeping. Apply with that in mind and the team can match you to the right conversation.

**Q: Do I need a CPA license to apply?**

**Q: Where are TK Partners' offices?**
A: TK Partners has offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake, WA, serving Eastern Washington. The application form lets you note your preferred office. Which office each role sits in, and any hybrid or remote options, is confirmed by the firm during the hiring conversation, so ask early.

**Q: What should I prepare before applying?**
A: Have your resume, any licensure or credentials, your preferred office (Walla Walla or Moses Lake) and your area of interest ready. Interest areas include tax, outsourced accounting or bookkeeping. Send what you have, and questions are welcome through the contact page before you start.
