## Track your refund without the guesswork

The fastest answer comes from the IRS itself. Use the official [IRS refund tracker](https://www.irs.gov/refunds), and keep your Social Security number or ITIN, your filing status and the exact refund amount from your return nearby. Our own [refund tracker page](/resources/refund-tracker) walks through the steps.

Delays usually trace back to a typo, missing information, an identity check, or a refund applied to a past debt. The IRS sets the timing, so we won't promise a date. If your status stalls, call your TK Partners preparer at either office and we'll help you work out the next step.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Client resources

**Q: Where are the TK Partners offices located?**
A: TK Partners has two offices in Washington: one in Walla Walla and one in Moses Lake. Together they serve clients across Eastern Washington. The firm was formerly known as TKCPA, so older paperwork and email may still carry that name. Phone numbers for both offices are on our contact page.

**Q: Is TK Partners the same firm as TKCPA?**
A: Yes. TKCPA is the firm's former name, and TK Partners is the same team of CPAs and client accounting specialists. If a letter, form or email still says TKCPA, it is from us. Call either office in Walla Walla or Moses Lake if you want to confirm before sending anything sensitive.

**Q: How do I send tax documents securely?**
A: TK Partners uses a secure, paperless process: you upload documents through a protected portal, your preparer reviews them, you e-sign the return, and we file it. You do not need to email sensitive files or drop off paper, which helps when harvest, crush or dinner service keeps you away from the office.

**Q: How can I check my federal refund status?**
A: Use the IRS Where's My Refund tool at irs.gov/refunds. Have your Social Security number or ITIN, your filing status and the exact refund amount from your return ready. The IRS sets the timing, so we can't promise a date, but if your status stalls, call your TK Partners preparer and we'll look into it.

**Q: Does TK Partners work with wineries, farms and restaurants?**
A: Yes. Wineries, agricultural operations and restaurants are industries the firm specializes in. Bookkeeping comes at a fixed monthly fee with no billing surprises, and the CPAs handle industry-specific questions like winery inventory and cost of goods, farm depreciation and grants, and restaurant payroll and tip complexity.
