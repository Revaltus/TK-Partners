## What information we collect

We collect two kinds of information, and the first is much bigger than the second.

### What clients share for accounting, payroll and tax work

To do the work well, we need real records. Depending on what you hire us for, that can include:

- Identity details, such as your name, address, date of birth, Social Security number or EIN
- Bank statements, credit card activity, payroll records and prior tax returns
- Tax documents such as W-2s, 1099s and K-1s

The specifics depend on your business. [Winery](/wineries) clients send inventory and cost records across vintages. [Farm and ranch](/agriculture) clients send budgets and grant or subsidy paperwork, so agricultural accounting privacy matters from the first upload. [Restaurant](/restaurants) owners send payroll and tip records. Whatever your industry, this is sensitive material, and we treat it that way.

### What the website collects

When you use our site, we may receive:

- Contact form entries, including your name, email address, phone number and message
- Emails you send us directly
- Standard analytics and cookie data, such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location [CONFIRM WITH FIRM: analytics tools in use]

Client information security in accounting starts with collecting only what the job needs. We don't ask for more.

## How we use what you share with us

Your information is used to do the work you hired us to do. That's it. Here is the full list:

- Preparing and filing your business and personal tax returns
- Keeping your books current and running payroll under your fixed monthly fee
- Year-round advisory conversations about tax planning and cash flow
- Answering your questions and responding to website inquiries
- Meeting professional standards and legal obligations that apply to CPA firms

If you use our [outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) services, outsourced accounting privacy in Walla Walla works the same way as everything else here. Restaurant payroll and tip records are used to run payroll and keep your books accurate, and for nothing else.

We do not sell client information. We do not use it for unrelated marketing. A fixed fee means no billing surprises, and this policy means no surprises in how your data is used either.

## Keeping your records secure

Our [tax process](/what-we-do/income-tax) is paperless and digital. You upload documents through a secure portal and e-sign returns and forms there, so sensitive records don't have to travel as loose paper or ordinary email attachments. If you run a winery, farm or restaurant, you're often sending files from a tasting room office, a shop or a back-of-house desk. We ask that you use the secure upload every time.

As CPAs, our team is bound by a professional duty of confidentiality. Carrie Shelton, Ellen Camp, Cameron Lewis, Erin Denney and Connor McCaw all hold the CPA credential. Access to your file is limited to the people who work on it.

For anyone searching for secure accounting services in Walla Walla, here is the honest version: we protect confidential tax and bookkeeping records with a secure digital workflow and limited internal access. ## When and why we share information

We share information in only three situations. [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

- With your permission. If you ask us to send records to your lender, attorney or another adviser, we will.
- With service providers who support our software and client portal. They are bound to protect the data and may use it only to provide that support.
- When law, regulation or legal process requires it, such as a valid subpoena or a professional obligation.

That's the whole list. Walla Walla CPA client privacy comes down to a simple rule: your information is never sold, and it doesn't go anywhere you haven't agreed to or the law doesn't require. These are our business accounting privacy practices, in full.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Privacy policy

**Q: Does TK Partners sell my information?**
A: No. TK Partners does not sell client or website visitor information, and client records are not used for unrelated marketing. Your bank, payroll and tax details are used to prepare returns, keep books, run payroll and answer your questions. They are shared only in the narrow cases described in this policy.

**Q: How do I send tax documents securely?**
A: Use the secure digital upload in our paperless tax process instead of ordinary email. Your documents go into a protected workflow, and you can e-sign returns and forms there too. Only the team members working on your file have access, so winery, farm and restaurant records stay with the people who need them.

**Q: Does this policy cover records I shared with TKCPA?**
A: Yes. TK Partners was formerly known as TKCPA, so this policy applies to information you shared under either name. It covers website visitors and clients of the Walla Walla and Moses Lake, WA offices. If you have questions about older records, contact either office and we'll connect you with the right person.

**Q: How do I stop receiving marketing emails?**
A: Use the unsubscribe link in any non-essential email, or contact either office and ask to be removed. Messages tied to your work, such as tax deadlines, engagement letters or payroll notices, will still reach you, because they are part of the service you hired us to provide.

**Q: How long does TK Partners keep client records?**
A: Retention follows professional standards and tax-record requirements, which means some documents stay on file for years after a return is filed. Ask your CPA about the schedule for your own file, and request copies of your records any time you need them. Records are not kept for unrelated purposes.
