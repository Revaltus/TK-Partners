## Year-round tax planning led by Erin Denney

Growing operations hit entity questions at predictable moments, like a new tasting room, a land purchase, or a second location. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Meet the TK Partners team

**Q: Is TK Partners the same firm as TKCPA?**
A: Yes. TK Partners was formerly known as TKCPA. The firm still offers outsourced accounting, tax, and advisory work from offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake, Washington, with a team of CPAs and client accounting specialists built on five values: Excellence, Leadership, Drive, Friendship, and Flexibility.

**Q: Where are your offices, and who do you serve?**
A: TK Partners has offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake, Washington, and serves Eastern Washington. Clients farther out work with the team through a secure digital process: documents are uploaded and signed electronically, so a trip to the office is optional.

**Q: How does the fixed monthly bookkeeping fee work?**
A: Bookkeeping is billed as one fixed monthly fee, so you know the cost before the month starts and never meet a surprise invoice. After a first call about your operation and your bookkeeping, payroll, or tax needs, the team quotes the fee in advance.

**Q: Do your CPAs specialize in wineries, farms, and restaurants?**
A: Yes. The team works with wineries, agricultural operations, and restaurants. That means winery inventory and alcohol compliance, farm budgeting, grants, and depreciation, and restaurant food cost, payroll, and tips are familiar territory, not something you have to teach a new accountant.

**Q: Who leads tax planning at TK Partners?**
A: Erin Denney, CPA, is the Tax Manager. She leads tax planning for closely-held businesses, including entity structuring and multi-year strategy for owner-operators. Tax work runs through a secure paperless process with digital document upload and e-signing, and advice is available year-round, not only at filing time.
