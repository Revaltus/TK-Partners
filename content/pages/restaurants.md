## Food cost and inventory numbers you can act on

Food cost and inventory are the two levers that decide whether a restaurant keeps its margin. When either one drifts, you rarely feel it until the month is over.

Clean books and regular reporting show where cost creeps up: a supplier price that jumped, a menu item that eats its own profit, inventory that disappears somewhere between the walk-in and the register. With numbers you can trust, you adjust a portion or a price on a Tuesday instead of finding out a quarter later.

Food service accounting in Walla Walla also means keeping up with food service regulatory and tax compliance. We keep those deadlines on the calendar with the rest of your books, so they don't land on you as a surprise. That is restaurant financial management in Walla Walla that does more than record the past. It points at your next decision.
