## Inventory and cost of goods tracked across every vintage

Wine is slow. Grapes become juice, juice becomes barrel, barrel becomes bottle, and the cost rides along the whole way. If the books can't follow it, the margin on each label is a guess.

Winery bookkeeping in Walla Walla starts with carrying costs through every stage: fruit, cellar work, barrels, bottling, and finally the sale. When a bottle sells, the matching cost moves with it. The payoff is a margin picture by label and by vintage, so you can see which wines are paying for the ones you love.

Harvest accounting for vineyards has its own rhythm. Invoices stack up, crews change, and nobody wants to think about the books in the middle of crush. Our [Virtual Bookkeeper](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) service keeps coding current through the busy weeks, so September never turns into a January cleanup.

Each month you see current financial statements, inventory and cost of goods that tie out, and a short conversation about what moved. Wine industry accounting in Walla Walla should read like a good tasting note: clear, specific, and no filler.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Accounting services for wineries

**Q: What does a CPA for wineries do that a general bookkeeper doesn't?**
A: A CPA who knows wineries understands that cost follows the wine from fruit to barrel to bottle to sale. We track those costs by vintage and label, keep inventory records in order, and support alcohol reporting figures. A general bookkeeper often records the spending but can't tell you which wines are actually profitable.

**Q: How is cost of goods tracked across vintages?**
A: Costs are carried through each stage, including fruit, cellar work, barrels, and bottling, and assigned to the vintage and label they belong to. When bottles sell, the matching cost moves to cost of goods sold. The result is a margin by label rather than one blended number for the whole winery.

**Q: Does TK Partners bill hourly for winery bookkeeping?**
A: No. Bookkeeping is a fixed monthly fee, so crush season and year-end don't produce a bigger invoice. You know the cost before the month starts. Payroll and tax work are scoped with you up front as part of the outsourced accounting arrangement, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

**Q: Can you help with tax planning for vineyard equipment purchases?**
A: Yes. Tax planning runs year-round, so a tractor, press, or tasting room build-out can be modeled before you buy, not after. We look at depreciation, timing, and your owner-operator structure, with input from Erin Denney, CPA, our Tax Manager, on entity structuring and multi-year strategy.

**Q: Where does TK Partners serve wineries?**
A: TK Partners has offices in Walla Walla and Moses Lake, WA, and serves wineries across Eastern Washington. Tax documents move through a secure digital process with upload and e-signing, so you don't need to drive in during crush. You can also meet with the team in person at either office.
