## Tax preparation and planning that runs all year

A tax bill that shows up in March was usually decided the previous spring. That's the pattern we see most on farms: a big equipment purchase, an expansion, a strong harvest, and then a surprise at year-end. Proactive planning flips the order. You ask before you buy, we model the impact, and the filing becomes a formality.

Our [Business & Personal Income Tax](/what-we-do/income-tax) service covers tax preparation, tax planning and year-round tax advice. It all runs through a secure, paperless process: you upload documents digitally and e-sign when the return is ready. That makes us a practical choice for business tax preparation in Walla Walla and for personal income tax CPA support, including tax preparation services across Walla Walla, WA and beyond.

Behind the planning is Erin Denney, CPA, our Tax Manager. Erin focuses on tax planning for closely held businesses, including entity structuring and multi-year tax strategy for the owner-operators of wineries, farms and restaurants. She helps you see two or three years ahead, not just to the next deadline.
